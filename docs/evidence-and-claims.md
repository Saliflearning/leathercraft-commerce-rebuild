## Excluded material

The repository does not contain the old source or database, credentials or row values, collaborator identities, raw course feedback, prompts, rubrics, peer evaluations, business records, product photography, third-party themes, or rights-unclear fonts and media.

## Review method

Historical statements were accepted only when supported by authenticated course records, recovered packages, or qualified team evidence. New capabilities are labeled as reconstruction work. Unsupported or ambiguous statements were either qualified or excluded.
